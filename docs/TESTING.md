# Device smoke test

Run before every tag, on the Jolla Phone 2026, from the shell, never from the IDE:

    sailjail /usr/bin/harbour-salama

Install the RPM that `sfdk check -s harbour` accepted. Each line must pass with no
workaround; a failure blocks the tag.

## Checklist

- [ ] Launches under Sailjail; first run shows the tutorial at once, with no page sliding in: "Learn where your tabs are" over the dimmed screen, and Start. Under it, a picture of a page and of the bar, the bar reading "Search or enter address". Start: the card fades, "Drag the bar up to see your tabs" lies along the head of the screen, and a glow goes up from the handle on the bar's edge, over and over. A finger on the bar puts both away; a drag up follows the finger, and let go short it springs back and the glow comes back. Past the threshold the sketched grid comes up, the words go to the foot, "Pull down to go back to the page", and the glow goes down from the upper third.

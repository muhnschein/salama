## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Multi-tab browsing with a grid of page previews; tabs, the active tab and the previews survive restarts.
- Dragging the navigation bar upwards opens the tab grid; the grid's pulley returns to the tab it names.
- Address shown in the navigation bar, tapped to edit in place, opening addresses or searching with a configurable engine.
- Back, reload, stop and share.
- History with search, and bookmarks with edit and remove.
- Private tabs that leave no history and keep no cookies.
- Downloads through the platform transfer UI.
- Settings: home page, search engine, desktop site mode, clearing history, cookies and cache.
- Cover showing the current tab's title and icon, with a new-tab action.
- Finnish translation.
